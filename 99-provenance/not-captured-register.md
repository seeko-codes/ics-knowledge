## Curriculum-practice-lesson meta-exercise: status

The "Practice Lesson: Our Curriculum" sub-stage (`ics-course/04-supplementary/06-practice-lesson-our-curriculum/`)
is a two-page meta-exercise, not a technique lesson: it deliberately imitates imperfect, real-world,
multi-source teaching (in contrast to the course's own curated lessons) so the learner practises keyword
collection, note-taking, and critical reading/listening against a fragmented set of resources. Its status:

- **Page 1 ("Curriculum overview")** is captured as lesson framing (objectives, meta-objectives, when to use
  it — recommended at the end of Fundamentals 2, where it doubles as that stage's checkpoint material), but
  its actual working materials (a keyword list, a summary document, and a technical reading report) are
  external Google Drive links, not inline content, and are not captured. The "lecture" the exercise is built
  around is a video, which per the lesson's own framing *is* the primary substantive material — its absence
  is structurally expected here, not a documentation gap, since the exercise is meant to be worked against
  fragmented external resources rather than explained in text.
- **Page 2 ("System preview")** is a short teaser previewing footage from the Summit stage as a "sneak peek";
  its own substance is entirely the (uncaptured) video excerpt, plus the icanstudy.com/how-it-works external
  link recorded above.

Net effect: the meta-exercise's *framing* (what it is for, when to use it, what skills it practises) is fully
captured; its *working materials* (keyword list, summary PDF, technical reading PDF, source video, preview
footage) are not, and were never going to be, since they are either external Drive-hosted files or the video
payload the exercise is explicitly built to be practised against.
