  - Feeling something is "obvious" without first passing through confusion may indicate bypassed learning.
  - Chunking level is determined by comfort with confusion + experience generating questions, not raw knowledge.
  - Chunking during pre-study differs from chunking during the main learning event (distinct process).
- **criteria**: 2-4 rule (2–4 items per chunk, EXACT); 4-level chunking-skill rubric (definitional, per level above).
- **examples**: TEPID mnemonic for chest pain (medicine) — demonstrates why mnemonics make poor schemas.
- **failure-modes**: mnemonics as weak schema (poor internal connection/intuitiveness); escaping confusion instead of examining it; conflating "obviousness" with "meaningfulness."
