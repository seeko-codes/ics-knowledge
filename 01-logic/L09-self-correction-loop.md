## Statement

A learning system improves only through closed loops over its own process: observe cues,
monitor against expectation, respond with an adjustment, and test again. Subjective signals —
effort, confusion, fluency, feelings — are diagnostic data about cognitive state. The standing
enemy is calibration error: the learner's model of their own knowledge and process is
systematically wrong in known directions.

## Core claims

- Metacognition decomposes into knowledge (of self, task, strategies), monitoring, and
  control/decision-making; it is the mechanism beneath every improvement framework in the
  corpus (Kolb's, marginal gains, feedback, audits).
- Cue utilisation is the operational lens: cue → monitoring → response. Prediction error
  between expected and actual outcome is the learning signal; guided reflection exists to
  surface it.
- The experiential cycle (experience → reflection → abstraction → experimentation) is the
  canonical loop shape; reflection quality and abstraction quality determine the value of the
  experiment. Reflection should outweigh abstraction; loops are scoped small (max ~3 topics)
  and run frequently — rate of experimentation × rate of reflection ≈ rate of progress.
- Improvement is incremental by design: 1% marginal gains, tracked and stacked, compound;
  "some marginal gains aren't marginal" — a 1% step can require genuine insight into the
  binding constraint (rate limiter).
- Diagnosis precedes remedy: symptoms are traced to root causes before technique changes
  (diagnostic-first principle; black-box two-step; learning triad: cognition, notes, revision
  must logically align).
- Calibration failures are systematic and named: illusion of fluency, illusion of explanatory
  depth, Dunning-Kruger overconfidence at low skill, mindmap-completion satisfaction. External
  testing (retrieval, feedback, teaching) audits what introspection cannot.
- Feelings map to cognitive states and are read as instrument data (the corpus's recurring
  meta-checklists: "how it can feel" ↔ "what it means cognitively"); discomfort of the right
  kind is desirable difficulty, not error (and underload discomfort is equally diagnostic).

## Independence

Without L09, the system is open-loop: techniques could be executed but never evaluated,
corrected, or personalised — no Kolb's, no marginal gains, no self-diagnosis, no calibration.
Nothing else in the basis closes the loop.
