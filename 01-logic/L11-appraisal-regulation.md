## Statement

How a learner appraises mistakes, uncertainty, difficulty, and outcomes gates what learning
behavior is possible. Appraisals are trainable: reframing (process over outcome, journey over
conclusion, locus of control), graduated exposure, and identity-level work keep the learner in
the productive zone; unregulated appraisals (fear of mistakes, certainty-seeking,
perfectionism, outcome fixation) block the very actions learning requires.

## Core claims

- Outcomes are outside direct control; processes are inside it. Directing energy at the locus
  of control reduces stress and improves the probability of the outcome ("success is a symptom
  of the right processes").
- Mistake appraisal is the growth-mindset mechanism: reframing via Conclusion → Process →
  Journey (CPJ) converts failure signals into process information (supported in-corpus by
  cited mindset-intervention research; strongest correlate of confidence/performance gains in
  the program's own learner data).
- Certainty-seeking blocks practice: "information over experimentation" — asking what-if
  questions instead of acting — is a fixed-mindset symptom. The value of pre-practice
  information is inverse-U: enough to attempt and to recognise correctness, not enough for
  certainty.
- Fear is zone-structured: comfort → fear → growth; graduated exposure and small experiments
  move the boundary; reversion under pressure is expected and managed, not moralised.
- Neurotic patterns (rumination, catastrophising, perfectionism) are managed as a system:
  prevention, planning, execution, measurement, management — with cognitive-distortion counts
  as a self-scored risk signal, and clinical-grade tools (chain analysis) for entrenched loops.
- Identity is the relationship between thoughts, behaviors, and emotions; thought-action
  fusion (treating thoughts as facts/acts) sustains learned helplessness; defusion plus
  small, attributable wins (marginal gains) changes identity without destabilising swings.
- Energy is appraisal-coupled: alignment of activity with values/goals generates energy —
  it is not simply a fixed reserve that depletes and is restored by rest; misalignment drains
  regardless of rest arithmetic.

## Independence

Without L11, the affective gate is unmodeled: procrastination's emotional forms, fear-of-
mistakes paralysis, perfectionism, and motivation collapse have no mechanism, and the
conditioning layer (L10) cannot explain why identical environments produce different behavior
in differently-appraising learners.
