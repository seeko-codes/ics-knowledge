## Statement

Knowledge is stored as a relational network (schema), not as a list of items. Retention and
usability are determined by the structure of that network: how densely and meaningfully an item
is connected, how well the structure fits prior knowledge, and how cleanly it is organised.
Isolated information decays; connected information persists and transfers.

## Core claims

- An item plus its context of relationships = a schema (LC73's formulation). Learning quality
  is schema quality.
- Two relationship types: analogous (this is like X I already know) and in-context (this
  relates to Y within the subject). Information with neither type is effectively unretainable
  at scale.
- Chunking — grouping items into meaningful units — increases the amount of retainable detail
  by making organisation cheaper for the brain; chunks connected by clean inter-chunk
  relationships form a backbone.
- Schema quality is a spectrum: relevance (items grouped for a reason) → internal connection
  (relations within chunks are explicit) → intuitiveness (structure leverages prior knowledge
  so it "feels logical" and needs minimal memorisation). Mnemonic-only groupings (e.g.
  first-letter acronyms) sit at the bottom of the spectrum: grouping without meaning.
- Structural quality is diagnosable from the map itself: branch counts, chain shapes, and
  connection patterns carry information about retention risk (2-4 Rule, single-node chains,
  terminal nodes — see encoding-failure-modes).
- A chunk structure is a hypothesis: it must remain revisable as understanding grows;
  "correctness" is fit-to-purpose and fit-to-prior-knowledge, not correspondence to a single
  right answer.
- Directional relationships (arrows: causality, influence, sequence) encode more than
  undifferentiated association (lines), because direction must be reasoned about to be drawn.

## Independence

Without L02, process claims (L01) have no substrate: there is nothing for deep processing to
build, no structural quality rules (2-4 Rule, intuitiveness, backbone), and no reason chunking
or mapping should work at all. L01 is about the operations; L02 is about the resulting
representation.
