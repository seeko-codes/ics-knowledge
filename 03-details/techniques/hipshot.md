## Definition and prerequisite

Hipshot is an advanced Bear Hunter System variation: rapid alternation between Aim and Shoot
rather than separate passes. It is essentially a faster method of Aim-Shoot cycling that exploits
the high cognitive load tolerance trained through Aim-Shoot practice and the ability to form
relational networks rapidly. Prerequisite: near-unconscious competence in traditional Aim-Shoot cycling. Attempting
Hipshot too early risks a loss of quality/accuracy; the explicit remedy is to revert to
traditional BHS if struggling. Like Aim-Shoot cycling itself, Hipshot "often occurs as a natural
byproduct of unconscious competence developing with Aim and Shoot individually," rather than
being deliberately learned as a discrete new skill.

## Practice procedure (6 steps)

1. Collect keywords as normal.
2. Perform the Aim step mentally to identify a hypothetical logic layer.
3. Immediately Shoot on the mental model of the logic layer to validate or correct it.
4. As clarity about the validity and intuitiveness of the logic layer is gained, commit it to a
   GRINDE map.
5. Repeat from step 2 until each layer has been worked through.
6. Perform the Skin step once the initial map is satisfactory and ready for refinement.

Correct use of Hipshot saves time by (a) drastically reducing the time spent on Aim, and (b)
reducing the number of iterations before arriving at a map ready for Skin.

## Three implementation tiers

### Standard BHS implementation

Uses all normal BHS techniques daily; a good starter schedule for replacing normal study with
BHS. Features: Aim always happens during prestudy and covers at least one week's worth of
material; Shoot happens before (if viable), during, and after main learning events; Skin always
happens after the main learning event.

**Time efficiency: 5/10. Difficulty: Low.**

Instructions: (1) perform Aim for one week's worth of material at the end of the previous week
(e.g. Sunday); (2) Shoot only the most basic and important concepts/relationships if time allows
after Aim — otherwise do this the morning of, or at any time before, the main learning event; (3)
continue the Aim-Shoot cycle during the main learning event, finishing the Shoot step; (4) if time
allows, do the Skin step at the end of the day, or alternatively at the end of the week for the
whole week's content (both effective — what matters is doing what is consistent; busier weeks may
require alternating between the two).

### Accelerated BHS implementation

An intermediate schedule for working independently of the slower pace of classes and lectures.
Features: Aim always covers an entire topic at a time; the learner works at a different pace and
order than peers; Aim and Shoot always occur before main learning events; Skin occurs at the end
of the week for an entire topic at once.

**Time efficiency: 7/10. Difficulty: Moderate.**

Instructions: (1) spend one weekend Aiming and Shooting ~60–70% of the content for the entire
next topic, for a single subject, giving a significant lead and reducing pressure to "keep up";
(2) every weekend, repeat the same process for a different subject, staying weeks ahead of the
formal curriculum pace across most subjects at all times; (3) during the week, complete the Shoot
for the remaining 30–40% of content before, during, and after main learning events — the goal is
to finish both Aim and Shoot for the entire topic within one week of the formal timing; (4)
perform Skin at the end of the week. Attending classes/lectures after already self-studying is
useful as a recheck, re-skin, and easy multi-order retrieval session.

Tips: use available resources including Google; don't worry about covering 100% of the
curriculum, since being far ahead leaves plenty of chances to pick up missed material; continue
weekly + monthly interleaved retrieval appropriate to knowledge type and order to find gaps,
maintain retention, and ensure comprehensive mastery; continue lower-order retrieval methods for
layer-4 details throughout, which should not take much time with effective encoding methods;
there should still be plenty of time each week for other subjects as needed.

### Ultra-accelerated BHS implementation

For students adept at both standard and accelerated BHS with high mastery of the core technique;
creates total academic independence from curriculum pacing, maximising free time and efficiency.
Features: Aim, Shoot, and Skin all happen before main learning events using self-study material;
curriculum-week time is used for interleaved retrieval and getting further ahead or developing
further skills; entire topics are studied in a single session as the norm.

**Time efficiency: 10/10. Difficulty: High.** "This combination is essentially impossible for
most learners who have not had substantial cognitive training."

Instructions: (1) on weekends, breaks, or other times without classes/lectures, Aim, Shoot, and
Skin the entire subject's topic; (2) continue this for each subject every weekend, building first
to a few weeks ahead for one subject, then a few weeks ahead for every subject, then several
weeks ahead for all subjects; (3) continue spaced interleaved retrieval, prioritising gap-finding
and perfecting knowledge mastery with remaining time.

Tips: because self-study speed should be much higher than curriculum pace, an entire next topic
for a subject should be completable in approximately one day or less; don't aim for 100%
coverage — aim for ~90% coverage to 90% retention, since there is plenty of time to cover gaps
later; attending classes/lectures after already self-studying is useful as a recheck and easy
multi-order retrieval session.
